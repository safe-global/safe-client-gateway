```ts
expect(

```

**Literal deployment addresses in service specs (R-002)**

Then when the package bumps a canonical address, the tests don't silently pin to a stale value.

`contract-analysis.service.spec.ts:1282` — the title reads "should omit `FALLBACK_HANDLER`" but the assertion is on the presence of `CONTRACT_VERIFICATION: VERIFIED`. Aligning the title to the observable outcome (as the reviewer suggested) makes the intent obvious without reading the body.

Purely cosmetic.
